| **07. Web/PWA Sync** | Laptop Browser | Web Memories | Upload on phone | New snap appears on web grid without manual refresh | 4.0 seconds |
| **08. Outro Brand** | Clean Slide | Black screen | Fade in | Large glowing TeleVault brand logo & CTA URL | 3.0 seconds |
