# MARKETING_AD_SPEC.md

This document outlines the design and technical specifications for the TeleVault 30-second video advertisement.

---

## 📐 Technical Specifications

* **Ad Duration:** Exactly 30 seconds.
* **Resolution Formats:**
  * **Primary (Vertical):** 1080 × 1920 (9:16) for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels.
  * **Secondary (Horizontal):** 1920 × 1080 (16:9) for YouTube and web banners.
* **Frame Rate:** 60 FPS (recommended) or 30 FPS.
* **Video Codec:** H.264 / MP4.
* **Target Bitrate:** 15-20 Mbps (for vertical 1080p).

---

## 🎨 Visual & Audio Style

* **Brand Colors:**
  * Deep Dark Blue (`#0B0E1B` background).
  * Electric Neon Yellow (`#FFFC00` highlight).
  * Glowing Cyan-Blue (`#00A3FF` cloud sync lines).
* **Typography:** Bold, clean sans-serif (e.g. Outfit, Inter).
* **Transitions:** Minimalist cuts, zoom-into-phone screen scales, fast but smooth sweeps. Avoid cheesy presets.
* **Audio Track:** Modern ambient electronic beat with subtle synthetic pulses or emotional cinematic strings.
* **Voiceover Accent:** Confident, clear, professional voice.
